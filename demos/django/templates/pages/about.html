About RoboCare Health

We started RoboCare Health because we saw hospitals struggling with the same problems year after year: staff burnout, preventable errors, and patients waiting too long for care. Robotics offers a path forward — not to replace caregivers, but to handle the repetitive, physically demanding, and precision-critical tasks that drain their energy.

Our robots are designed by clinicians and engineers working side by side. Every interface is tested with real nurses, surgeons, and pharmacists before it ships. We believe technology should adapt to workflows, not the other way around.

Clinical Validation

All RoboCare systems undergo multi-phase clinical trials. We partner with academic medical centers to publish peer-reviewed evidence demonstrating safety and efficacy. Our surgical platforms have completed over 8,000 procedures with zero robot-attributable adverse events.

Global Reach

From Tokyo to Toronto, our robots operate in 75 hospitals across 18 countries. We offer local service teams, 24/7 remote monitoring, and over-the-air software updates that continuously improve system performance.
